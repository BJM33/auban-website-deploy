EXECUTIVE FAQ

Answers to critical questions
from boards of directors and investment committees.

How do Auban Advisory and Auban Technology operate together?

Auban is a dual-platform firm operating under single-source accountability. Auban Advisory leads financial analyses, M&A due diligence, regulatory compliance, and architectural strategy. Auban Technology deploys senior engineering squads to secure, modernize, and operate mission-critical platforms. Your transactions eliminate friction between executive decision and engineering delivery.

Can you mobilize rapidly for urgent M&A Due Diligence deadlines?

Yes. Our transaction teams in London, Paris, and Amsterdam are built for the strict cadences of PE investment committees. We produce Red-Flag diligence memorandums in 10 to 15 business days with objective quantification of remediation CapEx and technical debt.

How does the Aude platform differ from traditional vulnerability scanners?

Aude does not seek to replace existing security scanners or agents. It adopts an "Ingest-Don't-Scan" architecture: ingesting configurations, policies, and telemetry to deterministically verify that critical invariants (FIDO2 MFA, air-gapped backups) are closed, binding audit evidence to SHA-256 cryptographic hashes.

What is your confidentiality and conflict-of-interest policy?

Every transaction or cybersecurity mandate is bound by strict NDAs and air-gapped working environments. Auban accepts zero vendor commissions, reseller margins, or referral fees, safeguarding 100% fiduciary objectivity.
